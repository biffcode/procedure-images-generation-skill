# Metaesthetics Procedure Visual Guide — DEFINITIVE v3
# Source: sample_images benchmark + Dr_Gueret_Procedures_Descriptions.pdf + user examples
# Updated: 2026-08-11

## GLOBAL RULES (ALL images)
- Resolution: 1920x1080, 16:9 landscape ONLY — never portrait stretched
- Background: warm taupe (#C9B99A) or neutral clinical white/grey
- Skin: ALWAYS clear, healthy, beautiful — ZERO acne, ZERO injection holes/marks, ZERO redness
- NO Metaesthetics logo on any cover image
- NO text/labels/numbers on any device or syringe
- NO watermarks
- Style: Phase One IQ4 editorial medical photography — sharp, warm, premium
- Patient: real human-looking face — natural pores, natural imperfections, NOT AI-smooth plastic
- Mood: premium aesthetic clinic — NOT hospital, NOT spa

## GLOVE COLOR RULES (critical identifiers)
- BLUE gloves: Toxine botulique, Hyperhidrose, Cryolipolyse
- BLACK gloves: Masseters (ONLY)
- PURPLE gloves: Skinbooster (ONLY)
- WHITE/LIGHT GREY gloves: Mesolift, Acide hyaluronique, Sculptra, PRP, Rhinoplastie, Gyneco, Hydratation
- NO gloves: Profhilo (product box shot — bare elegant hand)
- WHITE gloves: BBL treatments

## SYRINGE TYPE RULES
- Toxine botulique: TINY 1mL insulin-style syringe, ultra-short ultra-thin needle (barely visible)
- Masseter: TINY 1mL insulin-style syringe, short thin needle at jaw angle
- Skinbooster: Medium 1-2mL syringe, fine short needle, slightly out of focus in foreground
- Mesolift: Standard syringe, fine needle, held close to cheek
- Acide hyaluronique: 1mL filler syringe with fine needle or blunt cannula
- Rhinoplastie: Fine needle or micro-cannula at nose bridge/tip
- Sculptra: Standard syringe with needle or cannula at cheek/temple
- Hyperhidrose: Very fine short needle, grid pattern on skin area
- PRP/PRP cuir chevelu: NO syringe in main scene — show PRP tube with golden plasma layer
- Profhilo: NO syringe — show product BOX held in bare elegant hand
- BBL: NO syringe — BBL Hero white handpiece device
- LED: NO syringe — LED panel/mask/cap device
- Cryolipolyse: NO syringe — cryo vacuum applicator device
- Consultation: NO syringe, NO device

## PATIENT EYES RULES
- Eyes CLOSED: Toxine botulique, Masseter, Mesolift, Rhinoplastie, Sculptra, Hyperhidrose, PRP, Gyneco, Hydratation
- Eyes OPEN (calm): Skinbooster, Acide hyaluronique
- Eyes with BLACK GOGGLES: ALL BBL treatments (mandatory)
- No face shown: Cryolipolyse (body only), LED capillaire (scalp), LED cicatrice (body area)

## PATIENT GENDER
- MALE patient: Masseter (primary), Consultation (can be male or female)
- FEMALE patient: All other procedures default
